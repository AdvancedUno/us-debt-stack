Chapter 1

Ground level

One $100 bill is 0.11 mm thick. The first centimeter of the stack is about 92 bills: roughly $9,200. Each mustard band is a $10,000 strap.

Chapter 2

Human scale

A person 1.75 m tall: $1.6 million. A school bus: $2.9 million. A two-story house: about $7 million.

Chapter 3

City scale

The Gateway Arch, 192 m: $176 million. The Burj Khalifa, 828 m: $758 million. Still under a billion dollars.

Chapter 4

Mountain scale

Mount Everest, 8,849 m: $8.1 billion. Airliners cruise at about 11 km: $10 billion. That is 0.02% of the debt.

Chapter 5

Space

The edge of space, 100 km: $92 billion. The International Space Station, about 400 km: $366 billion, under 1% of the debt.
